Scan & Annotate PDF

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Scan & Annotate PDF scans paper documents into a PDF with your phone’s camera, then lets you read, annotate, organise pages, sign with a verifiable Signer ID, and convert PDF, Word, OpenDocument, Excel and PowerPoint files. An optional AI assistant, using your own AI provider account, can summarise and check documents.

Checking a signed PDF

A PDF signed with this app shows a Signer ID (20 letters and numbers in groups of four). To check it, open the PDF in the app and choose Sign > Verify, or open it in any PDF reader that shows digital signatures. Then compare the Signer ID with the one the signer gave you.

Privacy

The app has no accounts, ads or tracking, and your documents stay on your phone unless you use an optional online feature. Read the full privacy policy.

Deleting your data

The app does not create accounts and the developer does not store your data. To remove everything the app keeps on your phone, open Menu > About > Delete all app data, or uninstall the app.
